Be Honest & Accurate

Accurately represent your skills, experience, and availability in your listings. Don't exaggerate what you can deliver. If you're a beginner at something, that's perfectly fine — just be upfront about it.

check_circleDo

"I have 2 years of experience with Python and can help with scripting and automation."

cancelDon't

"Expert full-stack developer, AI specialist, and DevOps engineer" (when you're a beginner).

Honor Your Commitments

When you accept a barter request, you're making a commitment. Follow through on your promises. The escrow system protects both parties, but trust is what makes HourX work.

Best Practices

• Respond to requests within 48 hours

• Set clear expectations before accepting a request

• If you can't fulfill a commitment, communicate early and cancel properly

• Complete sessions to the best of your ability

• Leave honest reviews after every completed exchange

star

Leave Fair Reviews

Reviews build trust in the community. When leaving a review, be honest, specific, and fair. Mention what went well and what could be improved.

Fake reviews, retaliatory reviews, or reviews based on personal grudges rather than the actual service experience are prohibited and may be removed.
