Frequently Asked Questions

help

How do I sign up for HourX?

Click "Get Started" or "Sign in with GitLab" and authenticate using your GitLab account. Your HourX account will be created automatically with 5.00 hours of initial credits. No separate registration form is needed.

help

What are time credits and how do they work?

Time credits are the currency of HourX. 1 credit = 1 hour of service. You start with 5 credits and earn more by completing barter requests from other users. When someone requests your skill, you receive their credits upon successful completion.

help

What is the escrow system?

When you send a barter request, the requested hours are deducted from your balance and held in escrow. If the provider accepts and completes the service, the hours are released to them. If rejected or canceled, the hours are returned to you. This protects both parties.

help

How do meetings work?

Once a request is accepted, both parties can join a private video meeting room powered by Jitsi Meet directly from the request page. The room is unique to your exchange and accessible only to the sender and receiver.

help

Can I cancel a request after it's accepted?

Yes, the sender can cancel an accepted request. The escrowed hours will be returned to the sender's balance. However, frequent cancellations may affect your reputation on the platform.

help

How do reviews work?

After a barter request is marked as completed, the sender can leave a review for the provider. Reviews include a 1-5 star rating and a written comment. You can only review users you've successfully exchanged with.

help

Is HourX free to use?

Yes, HourX is completely free. There are no subscription fees, transaction fees, or hidden costs. The platform is designed to facilitate genuine time-based exchanges between users.
